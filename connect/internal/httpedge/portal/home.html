Client setup guide · Get your connection setup from your operator, then run:

anvil-connect login
  1. Open the URL shown in your terminal and sign in with your passkey or permitted login method.
  2. Enter the terminal’s code, review access, and approve.
  3. Keep Connect running. Use the printed API URL and your local API key in your SDK. Press Ctrl+C to disconnect.

Run Connect on the same machine as your SDK. Over SSH, that means the remote machine. Run the command again when your session expires.
