# Qwen3.5 122B

## Current status and review date

Immediate managed Primary `rollback`. A fresh dual-PRO TP=2 qualification is
retained as `no-promotion`; the earlier MXFP4 route is also `no-promotion`.
Review date: 2026-08-01.

## Immutable identity

- Current: `nvidia/Qwen3.5-122B-A10B-NVFP4` revision
  `98915d837c4e7c87ac8296d02e89de19b3207e6d`.
- Historical: `olka-fi/Qwen3.5-122B-A10B-MXFP4` revision
  `345839ea666a70f5035672f7c88afcba6281921f`.

## Tested hardware and topology

Single- and dual-RTX-PRO-6000 configurations on Fakoli Dark. The 2026-08-01
run used both equal cards in exclusive TP=2 over PCIe without NVLink; every
other inference workload was offline.

## Engine, quantization, KV, context, and concurrency recipe

Rollback NVIDIA vLLM 26.06 path: ModelOpt NVFP4, BF16 KV, 262,144 tokens, one
sequence, one image, MTP disabled. Historical MXFP4 used vLLM nightly,
compressed-tensors MXFP4 with Marlin fallback, FP8 KV, 131,072 tokens.
The TP=2 lane preserves the rollback's ModelOpt NVFP4/BF16-KV controls, uses
one admitted request, and changes only the distributed topology where possible.

## Evidence by measurement class

- `external-prior`: NVIDIA card and dated community single-card recipes.
- `functional`, `capacity`, `quality`: image/OCR, 10/10 tools, repeated
  protocol-v3, 128K/240K retrieval, and near-ceiling capacity.
- In the matched 2026-07-29 c1 lane, Qwen passed 240K retrieval and 20/20
  tools, measured 68.91 s TTFT and 60.3 tok/s decode at 231,426 prompt tokens,
  and passed 12/12 image attempts.
- In exclusive TP=2, smoke/JSON/30K retrieval/tools passed, the repeated gate
  passed intelligence 6/6, session 3/3, and tools 3/3, and a 125,444-token
  prompt measured 14.59 s TTFT, 8,570 effective prefill tok/s, and 65.0 tok/s
  decode. At the 32K lane, TTFT was 2.32 s and decode was 67.5 tok/s.

## Decision and promotion state

Immediate managed rollback for the human-gated Agents-A1 FP8 Primary. Laguna
S 2.1 and GPT-OSS Puzzle remain additional recovery profiles. Agents-A1 won
the bounded 262K comparison and subsequently passed the complete repeated
protocol-v3 gate. Documentation does not independently authorize another route
change.
The TP=2 result is additional topology evidence and does not replace or
auto-promote the existing single-card rollback profile.

## Failures and gotchas

Near-ceiling prefill is slow. Preserve BF16 KV, c1 admission, the one-image
limit, and no-video boundary. When video was enabled in isolation, the exact
NGC 26.06 image lacked an H.264 decoder; all video-containing corpus requests
failed before model inference. This is a runtime packaging failure, not proof
that the Qwen architecture cannot understand video. The MXFP4 result did not
justify promotion.
