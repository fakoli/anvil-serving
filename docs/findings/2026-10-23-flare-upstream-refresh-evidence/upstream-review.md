The known GLM selection-width export fix `22887c1` and page-table-extent change `eb770324` already precede our old `47581cca` source. They are not new remedies. The [upstream GLM defect note](https://github.com/NVIDIA/flare-local-inference/blob/d5fd9824593c2a70f6bdc001b39f16b4c5b3b0b4/docs/glm-batched-speculation-defect.md) also records a remaining timing-sensitive KDA/NaN failure at TP4/C16. That warrants keeping invalid-distribution failures, but does not establish the same cause at our TP2/C4. Its neighbouring-record answer category is a model miss, not the documented corruption defect.

A byte-exact Snapshot→Restore→Snapshot is insufficient to disprove #16: both snapshots can omit the same required draft page. Conversely, a draft-page omission does not prove target-logit corruption. Keep byte fidelity, complete state capture, numerical equivalence and sampled output repeatability separate.

Our protected bracket still lacks request-correlated attribution. No reviewed change has been measured on our rig as a remedy for its roughly 3-ms increase. Current upstream metrics can narrow a future run, but histograms cannot supply missing per-request timing retroactively.
