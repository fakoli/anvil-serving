## Retained evidence and limits

Candidate direct preflight and default, enabled, and disabled-thinking controls
pass. Baseline refused the synthetic 128K launch-code needle twice at 105,647
actual prompt tokens, while its other functional observations remain retained.
Baseline high-context, three-repetition quality, session, and multimodal gates
passed. Its summary 60-request canary formatting failed; its strict controlled
turnover completed 53/60 with seven count/length failures. All 60 responses
arrived without foreign markers; none of those failed populations can support
a performance claim.

Candidate startup observed both-rank PCIe IPC and NCCL P2P. It also retained
an IPC-seed autotune warning and four TileLang race warnings. Independent Sol
review refuted the cited races through guarded injective indexes, but that does
not permit a warning-free claim or use of `TL_DISABLE_DATA_RACE_CHECK`.

## Completed direct comparison, decision held

The eligible matched capacity cells completed 12/12 at the 4K target and 3/3
at 120K in both arms. Candidate versus baseline changes are respectively
-5.19%/-1.31% median TTFT, -4.91%/-1.45% median E2E, and +3.93%/+17.21%
median decode. These small populations are descriptive only and establish no
p99, service-tail, or universal runtime claim.

The exact 60-request controlled turnover gate completed 58/60 for the
candidate versus 53/60 for the baseline. One pre-recorded unchanged strict
repeat completed 57/60; no loop reruns were used. The candidate's original two
misses were clean `stop` responses with 33 and 31 rather than 32 words. Its
repeat includes 33-word `stop` responses at indices 20 and 36 plus a 499-word,
512-token length truncation at index 32. All canaries and responses passed.
The strict-turnover disposition holds the candidate from full qualification
despite comparative performance gains.
Candidate PowerShell marker
diagnostics are 2/3 because one response lacks literal `absolute`; review
labels it a false negative with semantic caveats; see the
[independent ledger](2026-09-09-glm53-ormandj-v042-evidence/independent-review-ledger.json).
The baseline's 3/3 marker responses contain invalid code, so this does not
establish semantic regression.

## Restoration boundary

The exact baseline registry, image, manifest, router, and topology were
restored. Direct and routed baseline preflight each passed 2/2; the router was
admitting and ready with zero active requests, and the restored unit had zero
restarts. P2P remains enabled. The candidate routed gate was intentionally not
attempted because strict turnover failed. See
[restoration](2026-09-09-glm53-ormandj-v042-evidence/restoration.json).

The final evidence bundle will retain native artifacts, redaction provenance,
all ten artifact roles, and the final decision. It will record current,
rollback, challenger, or no-promotion only after the campaign owner supplies
the completed gates and authorization.
