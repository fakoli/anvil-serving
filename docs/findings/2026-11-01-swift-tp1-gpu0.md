# Swift TP1 qualification on one RTX PRO 6000

**Qualification accepted, November 1, 2026.**

The tested SGLang/MTP3 recipe passed its retained protocol, context, image,
agentic, SWE pilot, stability, capacity and resource gates, with the limitations
below. This finding makes no public claim about current deployment, routing,
aliases, or client assignment.

## Measured serving envelope

The fresh final capacity population is ten one-repetition cells (72/72
requests), distinct from the earlier 60-cell matched historical comparison.
The tested identity is `ukisai/Swift-1.5-Qwen3.8-27b` revision
`b4c84d42903a8646b25857eb2827288d92ed85a4`, served by SGLang 0.5.19 image
`sha256:37bbbd3444732a464bbc68dee4fb0164e0ce9e18e2f027f3fc967f1152d3c262`.
It uses BF16 weights, FP8 E4M3 KV, FP32 SSM state, TP1, EAGLE MTP3 (top-k 1,
four draft tokens), 262,144-token context, evaluated interactive concurrency two, and a
16,384-token output reserve. At C1, median decode was 63.44 tok/s at 8K and 62.46 tok/s at 32K. The selected
C2 operating point improved aggregate output over C1, but its 32K median decode
was 13.43 tok/s, below the 15 tok/s soft objective; first-output p95 was 16.69
s, within the 30 s ceiling. C1 remains the latency-first option. Final C16 is
queued requested load rather than sixteen active requests: configured-derived
engine active capacity is nine while final C16 sampled running maximum was
eight, so it is not evidence of sixteen simultaneous active decodes. C1/C2/C4
cells have four requests, C8 has eight, and C16 has sixteen; sampled p95 is
descriptive, not a stable
production tail estimate.

The final resource observer reconciled 11,949 engine samples and 2,992 slow
samples with zero OOM deltas, zero identity changes, and no use of the protected
second GPU. Deep health passed after the capacity sweep.
